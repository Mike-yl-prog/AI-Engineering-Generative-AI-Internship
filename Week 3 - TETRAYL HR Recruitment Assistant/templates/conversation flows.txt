Conversation Design Principles

The Tetrayl conversation flows are designed around five principles:

Principle	Implementation
Clarity	Ask users for missing information instead of guessing
Consistency	Use structured responses for recurring recruitment tasks
Safety	Avoid inappropriate disclosure and unsupported conclusions
Human oversight	Keep final recruitment decisions with qualified humans
Robustness	Test normal, ambiguous, mixed-intent, and adversarial inputs
