For Tetrayl — AI HR Recruitment Assistant, you can answer the seven business questions like this:

1. What problem does the AI assistant solve?

Tetrayl helps reduce the time and effort involved in common recruitment tasks. HR professionals often need to review candidate information, summarize CVs, prepare interview questions, and answer repetitive recruitment-related questions.

Tetrayl provides an AI-assisted way to handle these tasks more efficiently while keeping human recruiters involved in the final decision-making process.

2. Who are the intended users?

The primary users are:

HR professionals
Recruiters
Hiring managers
Recruitment teams
Small businesses handling their own recruitment
3. What tasks should the assistant perform?

Tetrayl should be able to:

Summarize candidate CVs and profiles
Extract relevant skills and experience
Generate interview questions based on a job role
Answer recruitment-related questions
Help compare candidate information against job requirements
Draft candidate communication
Ask for clarification when information is missing
Handle inappropriate, irrelevant, or unsafe requests
4. How should the assistant respond to different user requests?

Tetrayl should identify the type of request and provide an appropriate response.

User request	Tetrayl response
CV summarization	Provide a concise candidate summary
Interview questions	Generate role-specific questions
Candidate analysis	Highlight relevant skills and experience
Recruitment question	Provide a clear answer
Missing information	Ask the user for clarification
Unclear request	Request additional context
Sensitive request	Avoid inappropriate disclosure and recommend human review
Final hiring decision	Assist with analysis but leave the final decision to a qualified human
5. How will prompt engineering improve response quality?

Prompt engineering gives Tetrayl clear instructions about what it should do, how it should respond, and what it should avoid doing.

Structured prompts can define the assistant's:

Role
Objectives
Scope
Tone
Response format
Constraints
Safety requirements

For example, instead of simply asking an AI to "review this candidate," a structured prompt can instruct it to focus on the candidate's skills, experience, qualifications, and relationship to the stated job requirements, while avoiding unsupported assumptions.

This makes responses more consistent, relevant, and easier for recruiters to evaluate.

6. What limitations should users be aware of?

Tetrayl has several limitations:

AI-generated responses may contain errors or hallucinations.
Candidate information may be incomplete.
The assistant should not make final hiring decisions.
AI can reproduce bias present in data or prompts.
Sensitive candidate information must be handled carefully.
Prompt-injection and adversarial inputs may cause unexpected behavior.
Human review is required for important recruitment decisions.

Therefore, Tetrayl should be treated as an HR support tool rather than an autonomous hiring system.

7. How can the assistant be improved in future iterations?

Future versions of Tetrayl could include:

Automated CV/PDF extraction
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Improved candidate-to-job matching
More robust prompt-injection detection
Bias and fairness evaluation
Candidate database integration
Authentication and role-based access
Recruitment analytics dashboard
Better handling of ambiguous and mixed-intent requests
Continuous evaluation using larger and more diverse test datasets

Overall objective: Tetrayl should evolve from a simple recruitment assistant into a more reliable human-in-the-loop recruitment support system, where AI handles repetitive work while recruiters retain responsibility for important decisions.
