Ambiguous Questions
Can I see a specialist?
Assistant

Based on the HealthConnect Clinic Knowledge Base, patients can select specialist consultations by appointment. The available specialist consultations vary, and patients should contact reception for specific information on the services offered. To book an appointment with a specialist, patients can choose from the available appointment times and dates, select their preferred clinic location where available, and provide required contact information. Patients should review appointment details before confirming. Please note that HealthConnect Clinic does not have real-time appointment availability, and I'm not able to book or confirm appointments. If you have any further questions or would like to schedule an appointment, I recommend contacting the clinic reception directly.
Can I change my appointment?
Assistant

I'm not able to book or change appointments on behalf of patients. If you need to reschedule or cancel an appointment, I recommend contacting HealthConnect Clinic reception directly. They will be able to assist you with any changes or cancellations.
Do I need documents?
Assistant

If you need documents, you should contact the clinic reception to inquire about the specific documents required for your appointment. They can provide you with the necessary information and let you know what to bring with you on the day of your appointment.
