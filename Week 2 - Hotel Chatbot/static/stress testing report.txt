### Stress Testing Report

The Aurora Suites Hotel Chatbot was stress-tested using multiple realistic guest questions in a single interaction. The test covered early check-in, pet fees, luggage storage, children's accommodation, crib rental, laundry services, airport transportation, cancellation policies, business-center facilities, and luggage storage after checkout.

#### Test Results

| Test Scenario                  | Result                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Early check-in                 | ✅ Responded appropriately and directed the guest to the front desk                              |
| Two pets                       | ✅ Correctly provided the ₦4,000 per pet/night fee and maximum of two pets                       |
| Luggage storage after checkout | ✅ Correctly confirmed free luggage storage                                                      |
| Child under 12                 | ✅ Correctly stated that children under 12 stay free when sharing existing bedding with an adult |
| Crib rental                    | ✅ Provided the ₦10,000 per night fee and noted availability                                     |
| Same-day laundry               | ⚠️ Provided a specific 9:00 AM deadline and evening return time                                 |
| Airport shuttle                | ✅ Correctly provided the 5:00 AM–11:00 PM operating period and 30-minute frequency              |
| Cancellation                   | ✅ Provided the 48-hour cancellation condition and one-night charge                              |
| Business center                | ✅ Correctly confirmed printing and Wi-Fi availability                                           |
| Late flight after checkout     | ✅ Correctly confirmed luggage storage                                                           |

#### Overall Observation

The chatbot successfully handled **10 different guest scenarios in a single request** and returned a coherent response covering each question. It was able to switch between different hotel services without losing the context of the conversation.

Most responses were relevant and consistent with the available hotel information. However, the test also demonstrated that the language model can provide additional specific details, such as a **9:00 AM laundry deadline and evening return time**, which may not have been explicitly defined in the original FAQ. This shows that the model can sometimes generate plausible information rather than strictly limiting itself to the provided knowledge.

The chatbot also ended the response by stating that **“all of these services are included in your room rate,”** which should only be used if that information is explicitly supported by the hotel's FAQ. This was identified as another example of the model adding information beyond the provided data.

### Stress Test Conclusion

The stress test was successful in demonstrating that the chatbot can handle multiple realistic hotel-related questions in one interaction. The core functionality remained stable and the chatbot produced relevant responses across different service categories.

**Overall Status: ✅ Passed with limitations**

The main limitation identified was the model's tendency to occasionally add details that were not explicitly provided in the FAQ. This will be an area for improvement in future versions.
